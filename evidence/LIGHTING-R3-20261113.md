# PlayMoru Chess Duel R3: lighting repair / objective A-B

2026-11-13 · Z890 RTX 5070 Ti, genuine Chromium/Chrome Vulkan ANGLE rendering (headless); 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 portrait mobile viewport simulated on desktop GPU.

## Problem and diagnosis
The public default Rome/Frost arena rendered with too-dark surrounding geometry and pieces; a phone viewport was noticeably worse. Original default Frost exposure 0.98, hemisphere intensity 1.2, fill 0.75, lamp 0.28, grade vignette 0.52, CSS vignette 0.22. Both vignettes compounded edges, but disabling PostFX reduced rather than increased the mean slightly, proving the chief cause was lighting/material/readability and portrait framing, not a single post-processing switch.

## Changes
- Frost daylight relighting: balanced warm sun and cool fill, stronger hemisphere/environment and camera-facing lamp, lighter environmental materials to lift faces/shields and hall.
- Raised exposure moderately (0.98 to 1.12); lowered grade + overlay vignette to avoid double-edge darkening.
- Brightened dark tile and frame materials, *lowered bloom strength and raised threshold* to protect pale tiles from blooming into white.
- No change to chess rules, animations, piece source/provenance or existing camera movements.
