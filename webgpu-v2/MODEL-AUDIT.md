Six rigged model payloads total: 59.3 MiB.
Four additional walk/idle/attack/death clips per role are loaded from original GLBs.
32 actors share the six original geometries and texture resources and use individual independent skeleton clones.
PBR factions differ via art-preserving material tints. No procedurally substituted or coarse characters.
Only Rome is bundled in V2 (about 61 MiB), rather than all three legacy eras (about 186 MiB).
KTX2/mesh compression should only proceed after screenshot comparison and rights/texture inspection.
